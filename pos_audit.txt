Coupon Status Review

The statuses Active, Inactive, Expired, and Scheduled are valid. Inactive should ideally indicate a manually disabled coupon, while Expired should indicate that the validity period has ended.

Date/status consistency should also be verified. For example, a coupon shown as Scheduled with a validity end date in the past would be inconsistent if the dates represent live production data.

The coupon "Free Shipping" is appropriate if the super shop supports online ordering or home delivery. If the system is strictly in-store, a supermarket-relevant promotion may be more appropriate.
